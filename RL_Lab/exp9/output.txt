CONCLUSION & INFERENCE:

3. SARSA learns a safer policy along the upper edge (17 steps, reward -17), avoiding exploration penalties and achieving higher online training return.
4. This highlights the classic tradeoff: Off-policy (learns optimal greedy policy) vs On-policy (learns optimal exploratory policy).
